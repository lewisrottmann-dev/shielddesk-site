ShieldDesk Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 20, 2026

ShieldDesk is designed as a local-first security desk app. This policy explains how ShieldDesk handles app data.

Data collection

ShieldDesk does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal data through a hosted backend.

Local storage

Readiness controls, evidence notes, training answers, local cases, message triage results, link check results, and report state are stored locally on the user's device. On supported native builds, app state is stored using platform secure storage.

Device authentication

ShieldDesk may use the device's built-in authentication features, such as Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode unlock, to protect access to local app data. Biometric data is handled by the operating system and is not collected by ShieldDesk.

Exports and sharing

Users may choose to export or share a readiness brief, local backup, or support bundle. Exported files may contain information the user entered into the app, including case notes or triage results. Users control whether and where those files are shared.

Analytics and advertising

ShieldDesk does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Support and feedback emails

If a user voluntarily emails ShieldDesk for support or beta feedback, the email address and message contents are used only to respond, troubleshoot issues, and improve ShieldDesk. The app itself does not collect or transmit this information automatically.
