ShieldDesk Support

ShieldDesk is a local-first security desk for suspicious message triage, link checks, local cases, support bundle export, and security readiness workflows.

Contact

For support, feedback, bug reports, or App Store questions, email: lewis.rottmann@kairneximpact.ltd

Beta testers can also use the ShieldDesk beta feedback page for a short testing checklist and email template.

What to include

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your operating system version
  • The ShieldDesk app version
  • The active tab or workflow, such as Desk, Plan, Training, Report, or About
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Whether a local support bundle was exported

Account access

ShieldDesk does not require a cloud account. App data is stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share a report, backup, or support bundle.

Pricing questions

For Solo, Team, Business, or Enterprise pricing questions, review the ShieldDesk pricing page or email support with the expected Mac seat count and deployment scope.

Important note

ShieldDesk supports security triage, readiness, and organization. It does not guarantee compliance, insurance approval, malware removal, or breach prevention.
