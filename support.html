ShieldDesk Support

ShieldDesk is a local-first cybersecurity readiness app for small businesses. It helps users organize security controls, evidence notes, phishing drills, incident playbooks, and readiness reports.

Contact

For support, feedback, bug reports, or App Store questions, email: lewisrottmann@gmail.com

Beta testers can also use the ShieldDesk beta feedback page for a short testing checklist and email template.

What to include

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • The ShieldDesk app version
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead

Account access

ShieldDesk does not require a cloud account. App data is stored locally on the user's device.

Important note

ShieldDesk is a readiness and organization tool. It does not guarantee compliance, insurance approval, or breach prevention.
