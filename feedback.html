What to Test

  • Open the Command, Desk, Plan, Training, Report, and About tabs.
  • Paste a suspicious message in the Desk tab and create a local case.
  • Run a URL through Link Check and export a support bundle.
  • Update a few plan items and add an evidence note.
  • Complete the phishing recognition drills.
  • Review the incident playbooks and readiness brief.
  • Try exporting or sharing the readiness brief.
  • Report any crashes, confusing screens, typos, or layout issues.
